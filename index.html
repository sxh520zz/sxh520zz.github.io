01ABOUT

A little about my work

Emotion is not just
what we say, but how.

I am an algorithm engineer at AISpeech Co., Ltd. (思必驰科技有限公司) in Suzhou, China, working at the intersection of human–computer interaction, affective computing, and speech foundation models. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Nagoya University, Japan (2022–2026), under the supervision of Prof. Tomoki Toda. My doctoral research focused on speech emotion recognition, multimodal emotion modeling, and emotion dynamics in conversation.

I am particularly interested in moving beyond discrete emotion categories toward representations that capture mixed, continuous, and context-dependent affective states. More recently, my work has expanded toward LLM-based emotional dialogue agents, controllable emotional speech synthesis, and continuous emotion-conditioned music generation.

04PROJECTS

From research to systems

Selected projects

PROJECT / 01

Emotion-aware Speech Interaction Agent

Nagoya University · Project Lead · 2026

Designed an LLM-based agent pipeline connecting user-input understanding, emotion-state modeling, response planning, emotional speech synthesis, and frontend interaction.

  • Used Qwen3.5 as an emotion planner to predict emotion category, intensity, TTS emotion description, speaking style, and response content.
  • Integrated structured-output validation and fallback handling with Qwen3.5 and CosyVoice2.
LLM AgentEmotion PlanningCosyVoice2
PROJECT / 02

Continuous Emotion-Controlled Music Generation

Nagoya University · Project Lead · 2026

Explored continuous Valence–Arousal conditioning for music generation based on ACE-Step 1.5 and dynamic emotion annotations from DEAM.

  • Prepared 15-second music segments and encoded continuous VA values as learnable emotion-control tokens.
  • Applied parameter-efficient LoRA fine-tuning in DiT attention modules.
ACE-Step 1.5Valence–ArousalLoRA
PROJECT / 03

LLM-based Emotional Dialogue Speech Generation

Nagoya University · Project Lead · 2025–2026

Developed a conversational emotion framework using Who, When, and What interaction information, with LLM-generated fine-grained emotion descriptions.

  • Mapped dialogue history and target text to emotion descriptions using Qwen.
  • Integrated emotion descriptions into the CosyVoice2 semantic generation stage and injected 3W representations into the flow acoustic module.
QwenCosyVoice2Context-aware TTS
PROJECT / 04

Multimodal Emotion Interaction for Virtual Humans

Tonghuashun · Project Lead · 2020–2022

Built a real-time emotion recognition and prediction system for virtual-human voice interaction and continuous dialogue.

  • Achieved 78% multimodal emotion recognition accuracy and 65% speech-only accuracy across four emotion categories.
  • Developed emotion-trend prediction from dialogue history to support interruption decisions and expressive feedback.
Multimodal SERReal-time InferenceVirtual Human
PROJECT / 05

Customer-service Emotion Polarity Detection

Tonghuashun · Project Lead · 2020–2022

Designed an emotion polarity detection system for 8 kHz telephone speech, supporting streaming and offline inference for service quality monitoring.

  • Developed supervised high-accuracy and unsupervised low-annotation-cost training solutions.
  • Reported up to 95% accuracy for the multimodal solution in the target setting.
8 kHz SpeechStreamingQuality Monitoring
PROJECT / 06

End-to-end Emotional Speech Synthesis

Tonghuashun · Research Contributor · 2021–2022

Developed an emotional speech synthesis system based on Tacotron, combining emotion labels and speaker-style representations for fine-grained expressive generation.

01ABOUT

A little about my work

Emotion is not just
what we say, but how.

I am a researcher working at the intersection of speech processing, affective computing, and human–computer interaction. I received my Ph.D. from Nagoya University, Japan, where my doctoral research focused on emotion recognition and modeling in speech and conversation.

I am interested in developing computational methods that move beyond categorical emotion labels to capture subtle, mixed, and context-dependent affective states. I also explore how these insights can inform expressive speech generation and more natural human–machine interaction.
